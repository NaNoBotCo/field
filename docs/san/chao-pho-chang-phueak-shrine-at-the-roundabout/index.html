ศาลเจ้าพ่อช้างเผือกที่วงเวียนChao Pho Chang Phueak shrine at the roundabout

A monument with a black inscribed stone and a small Lanna-style tiered-roof pillar with a gold emblem, at the Chang Phueak roundabout; believed to be the guardian-spirit shrine the roundabout is named for.
