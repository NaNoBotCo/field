ศาลพระภูมิสีทองข้างเสาไฟฟ้าA gilded spirit house behind the roadside poles

A small gold-and-red Thai spirit house with a peaked tiered roof stands on a tall pedestal against a corrugated metal fence, beside a hotel driveway; a second, reddish shrine base sits behind it and a yellow cloth is tied to the nearby utility pole. Two utility poles cross in front of it.
