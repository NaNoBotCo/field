ศาลพระภูมิไม้สีแดงทองริมทางเข้าRed-and-gold wooden spirit houses at a driveway entrance

Dark red lacquered wooden spirit house with gilt roof trim on a pedestal, a taller gold-spired one behind it, on a concrete pad beside a round clipped shrub and taro leaves. Next door to a restaurant with a woven-bamboo front.
